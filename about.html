About Me

Hello, I’m Omkar Pujar.

I am a final-year Robotics and Automation Engineering student with a strong interdisciplinary foundation spanning mechanical system design, motion control, embedded systems, and intelligent software development. My work focuses on building integrated solutions that combine precision engineering with smart automation strategies.

I have designed and developed a high-precision Cartesian coordinate robot and a hybrid quadruped robot with wheeled locomotion, gaining hands-on experience in kinematics, actuator integration, sensor systems, control logic, and real-time implementation.

In addition to robotics, I actively develop algorithm-driven automation systems, including Expert Advisor (EA) trading bots, where I implement structured logic, data-based decision models, and performance optimization techniques.

My interdisciplinary approach enables me to bridge hardware and software, creating intelligent systems that are efficient, scalable, and application-focused.

I am seeking opportunities in robotics, automation, and intelligent software systems where I can contribute technical expertise, analytical thinking, and innovation-driven problem-solving.
